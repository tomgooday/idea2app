# Idea2App — Australia Rollout Phases Spec

> Part 3 of 4 in the growth playbook. See also: `01-time-leverage-and-operations.md`,
> `02-au-target-segments-and-channels.md`, `04-conversion-funnel-and-measurement.md`.
>
> **Status:** Proposal / not yet actioned. This is a working spec to react to
> and refine, not a decision recorded in the PRD.

## Purpose

Sequence the channels and segments from
`02-au-target-segments-and-channels.md` into a phased rollout, so effort
builds in the right order — foundation, then organic, then community,
then paid, then PR — rather than everything starting at once and none of
it getting enough attention to work.

## Phase 0 — Foundation, before any promotion

Do these before spending time on any channel:

- Pick one avatar and one hook first. Recommended: small business
  owners/consultants, using the AU dev-cost comparison — the largest,
  most locally specific pain point (see `02-au-target-segments-and-channels.md`).
- Install PostHog before spending effort on channels, so it's possible to
  see which post or video is actually driving clicks rather than guessing
  (already decided per PRD Section 23, not yet installed).
- Add an email capture so free-tier visitors aren't a dead end (see
  `04-conversion-funnel-and-measurement.md`).
- Record one flagship asset — a real screen-recorded build, start to
  finish. One shoot becomes a YouTube episode, a LinkedIn thread, and a
  blog post simultaneously.

## Phase 1 — Organic engine (weeks 1–6)

- Cadence: 3–4 LinkedIn posts/week, 1 YouTube video/week, 1 SEO post/week,
  cross-posted to X.
- Every piece ends with the same soft CTA: "Free Step 1 + one prompt at
  idea2app.co."
- Watch PostHog for which single hook drives the most clicks; double down
  there and drop what's flat after two attempts.

## Phase 2 — Community seeding (from week 2, ongoing)

- Show up in five AU-specific groups/communities per week with genuinely
  useful answers, not links.
- Attend one AU meetup a month (Fishburners, Cremorne Digital Hub, Startup
  Grind) — mention the product once you're a known face, not a stranger
  dropping a link.

## Phase 3 — Paid amplification (month 2–3, once organic has a proven hook)

- Retarget visitors of the Steps and pricing pages on Meta with the DIY
  offer.
- Run Google Search ads on 5–10 high-intent AU keywords ("build an app
  without a developer Australia", "app development cost Australia").
- Budget rule: never spend more on ads in a month than organic sold the
  month before.

## Phase 4 — Partnerships & PR (month 3+, once there are 2–3 case studies)

- Pitch AU startup media/newsletters/podcasts with a customer story, not a
  product pitch.
- Ask happy DIY/PRO customers for testimonials and offer a referral
  incentive (e.g. a free PRO upgrade for a successful referral) — a
  compounding loop that costs no ad spend.

## Cadence summary

| Phase | Timing | Primary activity | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Foundation | Before launch | Analytics, lead capture, flagship content asset | All four items shipped |
| 1 — Organic engine | Weeks 1–6 | LinkedIn, YouTube, SEO, X | A repeatable hook identified in PostHog |
| 2 — Community seeding | Week 2+, ongoing | AU communities, meetups | Runs continuously alongside other phases |
| 3 — Paid amplification | Month 2–3 | Meta retargeting, Google Search ads | Organic has a proven, repeatable hook |
| 4 — Partnerships & PR | Month 3+ | Media/podcast pitching, referrals | 2–3 real customer case studies exist |

## Open decisions
